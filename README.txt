ENQUIRY FORMS
- Enquiries are prepared in the visitor's browser and copied to the clipboard when allowed.
- The site opens the WhatsApp chat without adding form details to a URL. Visitors paste,
  review and tap Send in WhatsApp; the site cannot confirm that a message was sent.
- No personal form fields are sent to analytics or stored on a website server.

IMAGE ASSETS
- Pages use resized, compressed hero-optimized.jpg and matara-optimized.jpg copies;
  the original JPEGs remain in assets/img. Regenerate the copies when source images change.
- Check remotely hosted Unsplash images before launch and replace unavailable URLs
  with licensed or business-approved images.

ANALYTICS AND GOOGLE ADS
- Configure the placeholders in assets/js/main.js: ga4MeasurementId,
  googleAdsId, and the conversion labels.
- The site uses the Google tag directly. Do not also install a second GA4 or Ads
  tag through Google Tag Manager unless you remove or coordinate this integration.
- Analytics and advertising consent default to denied. Connect a consent manager
  and call LFTTracking.updateConsent({analytics: true, ads: false}) (or the
  visitor's actual choices) after consent is collected.
- Events use page paths and fixed event categories only; names, email, phone,
  dates, destinations, budgets and free-text requirements are excluded.
- WhatsApp clicks and enquiry handoffs are intent events, not confirmed enquiries
  or bookings. Do not map them to a qualified-booking conversion. Import qualified
  leads from a CRM/offline source only after the business confirms receipt.
- Leave the qualifiedEnquiry label unused until a confirmed, privacy-compliant
  offline conversion process is in place.

BUSINESS DETAILS TO CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Verified starting prices (or keep “Quote on request”), destinations and daily itineraries.
- Confirmed included and excluded services for each tour.
- Vehicle types, passenger/luggage capacity, photos, accessibility, driver experience
  and languages, and whether airport pickup is actually offered.
- Genuine customer testimonials and permission to publish them.
- Final privacy notice and consent-manager behavior for the markets being targeted.

IMPORTANT BEFORE GOING LIVE:
- Replace sample stock photography with your own licensed/original photos.
- Confirm all suggested itineraries, inclusions, rates and business claims. No fixed prices are published.
- Test every page and WhatsApp link on mobile and desktop.
- Replace all tracking placeholders and test consent and conversion actions in GA4 and Google Ads.
- Enable SSL and verify the sitemap in Google Search Console.
